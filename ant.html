Acromyrmex Octospinosus

The Acromyrmex octospinosus is a type of leaf-cutter ant from Central and South America.

These ants work together as a colony, or "superorganism." Different ants have different jobs in the colony.

Jobs in the Colony

  • Majors: Protect the colony.
  • Medias: Cut and carry leaves.
  • Minors: Help protect and maintain the nest.
  • Minims: Help care for the fungus garden.

Farming

Leaf-cutter ants do not eat the leaves they collect. They use the leaves to grow a special fungus, which they eat.

Conclusion

Acromyrmex octospinosus colonies are impressive because thousands of ants work together to survive.
