Acromyrmex octospinosus

The Acromyrmex octospinosus is a type of leaf-cutter ant from Central and South America.

These ants work together as a colony. Each ant has a different job depending on its size.

Ant Jobs

  • Majors: Protect the colony.
  • Medias: Cut and carry leaves.
  • Minors: Help protect and maintain the nest.
  • Minims: Help care for the fungal garden.

Leaf-cutter ants do not actually eat the leaves they collect. They use the leaves to grow a fungus that they eat.

Conclusion

The Acromyrmex octospinosus shows how a group of simple individuals can work together to form a very complex colony.
